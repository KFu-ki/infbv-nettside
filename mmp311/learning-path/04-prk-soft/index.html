PRK and SOFT: movement, attention and interpretation

PRK locates movement between problem, risk and crisis. SOFT challenges what the first situation analysis has made visible.

PRK

PRK is not a linear escalation ladder. An issue may be clarified before it becomes a problem. A problem may create risk without becoming a crisis. Crisis response may in turn produce new problems and risks. The framework helps students ask where pressure is located and what must change.

SOFT

SOFT tests the analysis across social conditions, opinions and expectations, framing, transparency and organisational conditions. The lens asks whose exposure and knowledge are visible, how the matter is framed, what the organisation can explain, and whether it has authority and capacity to change.

Framing, media logic and mediatization

Framing selects aspects of a matter and connects them to causes, responsibility, consequences and remedies. Media twist describes concrete selection and presentation techniques. Media logic concerns technological, aesthetic and institutional working conditions. Mediatization concerns longer-term institutional adaptation. These are distinct analytical levels, not a single documented causal chain.

Student activity

Apply PRK and SOFT to the same case. First locate the movement. Then identify what the initial account misses because of social position, framing, transparency or organisational limits.
