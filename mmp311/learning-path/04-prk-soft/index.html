PRK and SOFT: movement, attention and interpretation

MMP311 · Version 1.1 · Last academically revised 19 November 2026 · Responsible academic: Kristian Fuglseth

PRK locates conditional movement between problem, risk and crisis. SOFT challenges what the first situation analysis has made visible.

Issue detection before PRK

An issue stands before the PRK triad. It may be clarified without becoming a problem. If the matter exposes an expectation gap, material harm or sustained conflict, it may reveal or produce a problem. A problem may create risk without becoming a crisis, while crisis response may create new problems and risks.

SOFT as a critical lens

The resource uses SOFT as the name of a teaching lens rather than as a strict letter-by-letter initialism. It tests social conditions, opinions and expectations, framing, transparency and organisational conditions. The lens asks whose exposure and knowledge are visible, which interpretation dominates, what the organisation can explain, and whether it has authority and capacity to change.

Media distortion, framing, media logic and mediatization

These concepts operate at different analytical levels. Media distortion concerns concrete presentation techniques. Framing concerns selection, problem definition, causal interpretation, responsibility and remedies. Media logic concerns technological, aesthetic and institutional working conditions. Mediatization concerns longer-term changes in the relationship between media and other institutions. The levels can be connected analytically, but they do not constitute a single documented causal chain.

Student activity

Apply PRK and SOFT to the same case. First locate the movement. Then identify what the first account misses because of social position, framing, transparency or organisational limits.
