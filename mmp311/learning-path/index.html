MMP311 learning path

Seven connected learning resources combine theory, course reading, author-developed frameworks, cases and student activities. The pages can be revised independently.

8 February, 09:00–15:00

Risk society

Risk is not only a measurable property of a hazard. Risk also depends on what a society values, what experts can make visible, what institutions are prepared to recognise, and which consequences receive public attention.

11 February, 09:00–12:00

Risk communication

Risk communication is knowledge work under conditions where facts, uncertainty, values and practical action must be brought into relation.

16 February, 09:00–12:00

Issues management

An issue can be pointed out before it becomes a problem. Early issue work asks whether a development, expectation or disagreement requires clarification, change or dialogue.

18 February, 09:00–12:00

PRK and SOFT

PRK locates movement between problem, risk and crisis. SOFT challenges what the first situation analysis has made visible.

23 February, 09:00–12:00

FIVER

FIVER organises issue and risk work as a recurring process. It is an author-developed teaching and analytical framework, not a claim of predictive or empirical validation.

25 February, 09:00–12:00

Crisis communication

Crisis communication is part of crisis management. Information affects safety, coordination, legitimacy and the organisation’s capacity to act.

9 March, 13:15–15:15

Academic writing

Academic analysis connects a precise question, a defined material, concepts that change what can be seen, and conclusions that do not exceed the evidence.
