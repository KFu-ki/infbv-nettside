MMP311/Glossary

Glossary

Core concepts used across issue, risk and crisis analysis.

Issue
A matter becomes an issue when an actor makes conduct, consequences, values or responsibility relevant before the organisation has established that a problem exists.
Problem
A documented condition that obstructs valued outcomes or creates an expectation gap requiring investigation, clarification or change.
Risk
Possible future consequences considered together with uncertainty, exposure, vulnerability and what actors value.
Crisis
A situation that threatens valued outcomes, compresses decision time and challenges ordinary organisational capacity.
Public
A group that forms around a recognised consequence, shared concern or contested value. A public is not identical to a predefined target group.
Framing
Selection and organisation of elements into an interpretation of a problem, causes, evaluations and possible responses.
Media distortion
Presentational operations such as sharpening, simplification, polarisation, intensification, concretisation and personification.
Mediatization
Longer-term changes in relationships between media and other institutions that alter communicative conditions and organisational practices.
PRK
An analytical model for conditional movement among problem, risk and crisis. The model does not assume automatic escalation.
SOFT
A reflexive model for testing social and organisational assumptions, missing publics and blind spots in a situation analysis.
FIVER
A five-stage framework for understanding, identifying, evaluating, changing and responding to issues, possible problems and risks.
